The challenge

Why telehealth transcription is different

Virtual consultations introduce documentation challenges that standard transcription services aren't built for.

🎙
Poor audio quality
Telehealth calls suffer from compression artefacts, background noise, and unstable connections — requiring specialist transcriptionists, not generic software.
🌐
Accent variability
Both clinicians and patients join from diverse geographies. Automated tools struggle. Human experts trained in multiple accents do not.
⏱
High volume, fast turnaround
Telehealth platforms process dozens to hundreds of consultations daily. Documentation must keep pace without compromising accuracy.
🔒
Strict compliance requirements
Every transcript contains protected health information (PHI). HIPAA compliance is non-negotiable — and so is the Business Associate Agreement.

FAQ

Common questions from telehealth providers

Is Vishwa fully HIPAA-compliant for telehealth transcription?
Yes. All workflows are designed to meet HIPAA requirements for handling protected health information. We provide a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) before any files are processed. Our data infrastructure uses 256-bit AES encryption in transit and at rest.
What audio quality is required for accurate transcription?
Our transcriptionists are trained to work with compressed, low-bitrate, and noisy telehealth audio. While better audio quality always helps, we routinely achieve 99%+ accuracy on standard telehealth platform recordings including Zoom, Teams, and proprietary platforms.
What is the standard turnaround time?
Our standard SLA is 24 hours for most file lengths. We also offer a 12-hour priority turnaround for urgent documentation needs, and same-day service is available for critical cases. Custom SLAs are available for enterprise clients.
Which EHR systems does Vishwa integrate with?
We deliver output in formats compatible with all major EHR systems including Epic, Cerner, DrChrono, Practo, and custom platforms. We support HL7, FHIR-compatible formats, and direct API integration on enterprise plans.
Can Vishwa handle multi-speaker telehealth consultations?
Yes. Multi-speaker identification is a core feature. Our transcripts clearly label each speaker — clinician, patient, interpreter, family member — ensuring clinical records are accurate and medico-legally sound.
